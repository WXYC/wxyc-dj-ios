Underplayed Gems — rate-based B+C+D blend

A personalized feed of albums worth pulling off the shelf, computed from three independent rate-based signals so the math doesn't tilt toward whatever the station played most this month. Adaptive weights let you steer toward catalog-discovery, your-own-drift, or churn from current rotation.

B · Catalog rarity — albums the whole station plays infrequently. Long-tail catalog the LP shelves are hiding. C · Your drift — albums you haven't aired in a while, relative to your own history. Personal cold spots. D · Rotation tenure — rotation albums that have been on the shelf a long time and are aging out. Last call.

Each preset shifts how much each signal contributes to the rank. The chips that drove the rank stay visible per card — DJs need to know why a recommendation surfaced, not just that it did.

  • Tap the + on any card to expand supporting context

GET /djs/{id}/plays?since=...&limit=...
  // your history — feeds signal C

GET /djs/{id}/play-stats?window=...
  // your top-played, for compare context

GET /library/query?sort=plays_per_year_asc
              &include_rotation=true
  // long-tail candidates — feeds B + D

Notes

  • Phase B + D ship as soon as ADR 0006 lands (catalog-side only).
  • Phase C requires the per-DJ play feed.
  • Rotation plays included by default at weight 0.75 per Q8 grilling — rotation rotation-plays don't fully discount a record's rarity score, but they pull it down some.
  • Explanation chips are part of the surface, not an afterthought.

Per-album rotation tenure (signal D) is only defined for albums currently on rotation; "n/a" gems are catalog-only candidates and rank purely on B+C.

# Per-album play history is a first-class API surface, parallel to per-DJ plays

The Album Detail screen gains a histogram of station-wide plays of the album over time, sized and shaped after Apple Health's chart surfaces. The histogram exists on the same screen that already carries cover art, tracklist, condition state, and LML enrichment — the new section answers "what is this album's life at WXYC?" alongside the catalog and metadata answers the page already gives.

The backend gets a dedicated resource — `GET /library/{album_id}/play-stats` — that returns pre-aggregated year and month buckets in one response, plus first-played-at / last-played-at / total-plays summary fields. Pre-aggregation matters: iOS would otherwise re-bucket potentially hundreds of rows on every Album Detail open, on mobile, after a network round trip. The endpoint mirrors the shape of ADR 0006's `/djs/{id}/play-stats` (per-DJ) — same flowsheet substrate, different filter (`album_id` vs `dj_id`), parallel ADR rather than fold-in because the consumer-facing contracts and authorization profiles differ (per-DJ stats carry per-user privacy implications; per-album stats are intrinsically station-wide).

Scope: station-wide only. The album-detail surface is a programming-decision surface (queueing, condition, review) where the station's relationship with the album is the relevant context — "this album peaked in 2017, hasn't aired since 2022" changes a queueing call in a way that "I personally played it twice in 2023" does not. The per-DJ story for the same album is answered by Bin Maturity badges and the Top Played fold-in on Diversity Readout (Q15a) — those surfaces exist for that question; this one doesn't need to duplicate them.

iOS rendering uses Swift Charts with `.chartScrollableAxes(.horizontal)` + `.chartXVisibleDomain(length:)` + `.chartScrollTargetBehavior(.valueAligned)` — Apple Health's snap-aligned momentum scroll, available on iOS 17+ (this app targets 18.4+). A segmented Year / Month toggle sits above the chart; the default granularity is span-based — first-play to today > 5 years → Year, otherwise → Month. Year and Month are the only granularities; Quarter and Week were considered and rejected (Quarter is an analyst's unit, Week is too fine for sparse album-play data). The release year, when greater than the first-play year, renders as a dashed `RuleMark` annotated "Released YYYY" — the marker is intentionally suppressed when release year ≤ first-play year because the common case (album released before WXYC airs it) would overlap the first bar or sit alone before any data, adding chrome with no insight. Pre-release plays, when present, are not recolored — the chart's role is frequency representation, not status indication.

## Consequences

- **One new endpoint, no new schema.** `GET /library/{album_id}/play-stats` consumes the existing `flowsheet_entries.album_id` index (already in place for other queries). No migration. Cache-friendly: 60s TTL keyed on `album_id`, matching the staleness posture of per-DJ stats (ADR 0006) and Search Plays (ADR 0004) — the read rate (every Album Detail open) is far higher than the write rate (one row per actual play), and 60-second-stale aggregates are imperceptible to DJs.
- **Drill-in is deferred to v2.** Tapping a bar in v1 shows a tooltip ("2018 · 12 plays") — no navigation. Drill-in would target a paginated raw-rows list (`GET /library/{album_id}/plays`) that's effectively a filtered view over the same `FlowsheetV2TrackEntry[]` shape rendered by Search Plays (ADR 0004); once that surface exists, the drill-in becomes natural. Shipping tooltips-only in v1 defers both the BS pagination endpoint and the iOS row-list surface without losing v1 value (the histogram's primary signal — peaks, gaps, trends — is read from shape, not from row-level detail).
- **Empty-state behavior.** When `total_plays == 0` (album exists in catalog but never aired), the chart is replaced by a single-line caption "Not yet played at WXYC" — preserves section discoverability without wasting vertical space on an empty chart. When `total_plays > 0` but the album is new enough that Month view renders 2–3 bars, the chart honors the span-based default strictly rather than papering over sparseness — accurate is better than padded, and the always-rendered total caption ("47 plays since Aug 14, 2017 · last played Mar 22, 2024") contextualizes the data regardless of granularity.
- **Granularity-toggle consistency** with the Search Plays histogram (ADR 0004). Same Year/Month options, same scroll model, same span-based default rule. DJs read both histograms the same way; mixed-granularity conventions across the app would be a confusion tax.
- **Best-effort interaction with LML.** Release year comes from `/proxy/metadata/album` (LML), which is best-effort per project CLAUDE.md. On LML 404 / decode failure / rate-limit, the `RuleMark` is suppressed and the chart renders without the marker — same graceful posture as the existing LML extras on Album Detail. The `/library/{album_id}/play-stats` call itself is independent of LML and not subject to its availability.
- **Mirrors needed.** Backend-Service gets a mirror ADR. No dj-site, semantic-index, LML, or wxyc-shared mirrors required beyond api.yaml documentation (which BS owns as part of its mirror).
- **Reversibility.** Decision is cheap to reverse — pull the endpoint, remove the histogram section, and Album Detail returns to its v1 shape. No schema migration to roll back.
- **Verification needed before shipping.** Manual smoke test on a representative album set: one new release (Month default, sparse), one mid-catalog release (Month default, dense), one long-tail catalog release (Year default), one never-played catalog row (empty-state caption), one album with a pre-release play (RuleMark visible). LML failure injection on at least one of these to confirm the marker is suppressed gracefully.

# Per-album play history is a first-class API surface, parallel to per-DJ plays

The Album Detail screen gains a histogram of station-wide plays of the album over time, sized and shaped after Apple Health's [chart surfaces](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/charts). The histogram exists on the same screen that already carries cover art, tracklist, condition state, and [LML](https://github.com/WXYC/library-metadata-lookup) enrichment — the new section answers "what is this album's life at WXYC?" alongside the catalog and metadata answers the page already gives. Cross-doc context: this is iOS [pick #15](../sequencing.md#phase-3--personal-stats--profiles) under [ADR 0008 in the cross-repo doc](../cross-repo-adrs.md#adr-0008--per-album-play-history-is-a-first-class-api-surface-parallel-to-per-dj-plays).

The backend gets a dedicated resource — `GET /library/{album_id}/play-stats` — that returns pre-aggregated year and month buckets in one response, plus first-played-at / last-played-at / total-plays summary fields. Pre-aggregation matters: iOS would otherwise re-bucket potentially hundreds of rows on every Album Detail open, on mobile, after a network round trip. The endpoint mirrors the shape of [ADR 0006](../cross-repo-adrs.md#adr-0006--per-dj-play-history-is-a-first-class-api-surface-not-a-search-workaround)'s `/djs/{id}/play-stats` (per-DJ) — same flowsheet substrate, different filter (`album_id` vs `dj_id`), parallel ADR rather than fold-in because the consumer-facing contracts and authorization profiles differ (per-DJ stats carry per-user privacy implications; per-album stats are intrinsically station-wide). Backend ticket: [BS-31](../bs-work-inventory.md#bs-31-get-libraryalbum_idplay-stats-endpoint).

Scope: station-wide only. The album-detail surface is a programming-decision surface (queueing, condition, review) where the station's relationship with the album is the relevant context — "this album peaked in 2017, hasn't aired since 2022" changes a queueing call in a way that "I personally played it twice in 2023" does not. The per-DJ story for the same album is answered by Bin Maturity badges (the [pick #11](../sequencing.md#phase-3--personal-stats--profiles) badge surface, backed by `GET /djs/{id}/has-played` per ADR 0006) and the Top Played fold-in on Diversity Readout (the Albums axis added per [the ADR 0006 amendment](../cross-repo-adrs.md#amendment--albums-as-a-sixth-coequal-axis-on-diversity-readout)) — those surfaces exist for that question; this one doesn't need to duplicate them.

iOS rendering uses [Swift Charts](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/charts) with `.chartScrollableAxes(.horizontal)` + `.chartXVisibleDomain(length:)` + `.chartScrollTargetBehavior(.valueAligned)` — Apple Health's snap-aligned momentum scroll, available on iOS 17+ (this app targets 18.4+ per [project CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md)). A segmented Year / Month toggle sits above the chart; the default granularity is span-based — first-play to today > 5 years → Year, otherwise → Month. Year and Month are the only granularities; Quarter and Week were considered and rejected (Quarter is an analyst's unit, Week is too fine for sparse album-play data). The release year, when greater than the first-play year, renders as a dashed `RuleMark` annotated "Released YYYY" — the marker is intentionally suppressed when release year ≤ first-play year because the common case (album released before WXYC airs it) would overlap the first bar or sit alone before any data, adding chrome with no insight. Pre-release plays, when present, are not recolored — the chart's role is frequency representation, not status indication. Interactive UX prototype: [`docs/prototypes/diversity-readout.html`](../prototypes/diversity-readout.html) (the Diversity Readout drill-in lands on this Album Detail screen, which carries this histogram).

## Consequences

- **One new endpoint, no new schema.** `GET /library/{album_id}/play-stats` consumes the existing [`flowsheet_entries`](https://github.com/WXYC/Backend-Service/blob/main/shared/database/src/schema.ts) `.album_id` index (already in place for other queries). No migration. Cache-friendly: 60s TTL keyed on `album_id`, matching the staleness posture of per-DJ stats ([ADR 0006](../cross-repo-adrs.md#adr-0006--per-dj-play-history-is-a-first-class-api-surface-not-a-search-workaround)) and Search Plays ([ADR 0004](./0004-search-plays-flowsheet-builder.md), [ADR 0009 in the cross-repo doc](../cross-repo-adrs.md#adr-0009--flowsheet-archive-search-is-a-distinct-ios-mode-with-a-reusable-structured-filter-builder)) — the read rate (every Album Detail open) is far higher than the write rate (one row per actual play), and 60-second-stale aggregates are imperceptible to DJs.
- **Drill-in is deferred to v2.** Tapping a bar in v1 shows a tooltip ("2018 · 12 plays") — no navigation. Drill-in would target a paginated raw-rows list (`GET /library/{album_id}/plays`) that's effectively a filtered view over the same [`FlowsheetV2TrackEntry[]`](https://github.com/WXYC/wxyc-shared/blob/main/api.yaml) shape rendered by [Search Plays](./0004-search-plays-flowsheet-builder.md); once that surface exists, the drill-in becomes natural. Shipping tooltips-only in v1 defers both the BS pagination endpoint and the iOS row-list surface without losing v1 value (the histogram's primary signal — peaks, gaps, trends — is read from shape, not from row-level detail). Deferral is captured in [sequencing.md's "What's NOT in v1"](../sequencing.md#whats-not-in-v1).
- **Empty-state behavior.** When `total_plays == 0` (album exists in catalog but never aired), the chart is replaced by a single-line caption "Not yet played at WXYC" — preserves section discoverability without wasting vertical space on an empty chart. When `total_plays > 0` but the album is new enough that Month view renders 2–3 bars, the chart honors the span-based default strictly rather than papering over sparseness — accurate is better than padded, and the always-rendered total caption ("47 plays since Aug 14, 2017 · last played Mar 22, 2024") contextualizes the data regardless of granularity.
- **Granularity-toggle consistency** with the [Search Plays histogram](./0004-search-plays-flowsheet-builder.md). Same Year/Month options, same scroll model, same span-based default rule. DJs read both histograms the same way; mixed-granularity conventions across the app would be a confusion tax.
- **Best-effort interaction with [LML](https://github.com/WXYC/library-metadata-lookup).** Release year comes from `/proxy/metadata/album` (LML), which is best-effort per [project CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md). On LML 404 / decode failure / rate-limit, the `RuleMark` is suppressed and the chart renders without the marker — same graceful posture as the existing LML extras on Album Detail. The `/library/{album_id}/play-stats` call itself is independent of LML and not subject to its availability.
- **Mirrors needed.** [Backend-Service](https://github.com/WXYC/Backend-Service) gets a mirror ADR (see [`Backend-Service/docs/adr/0009-per-album-play-stats.md`](https://github.com/WXYC/Backend-Service/blob/main/docs/adr/0009-per-album-play-stats.md)). No [dj-site](https://github.com/WXYC/dj-site), [semantic-index](https://github.com/WXYC/semantic-index), [LML](https://github.com/WXYC/library-metadata-lookup), or [wxyc-shared](https://github.com/WXYC/wxyc-shared) mirrors required beyond [api.yaml](https://github.com/WXYC/wxyc-shared/blob/main/api.yaml) documentation (which BS owns as part of its mirror).
- **Reversibility.** Decision is cheap to reverse — pull the endpoint, remove the histogram section, and Album Detail returns to its v1 shape. No schema migration to roll back.
- **Verification needed before shipping.** Manual smoke test on a representative album set: one new release (Month default, sparse), one mid-catalog release (Month default, dense), one long-tail catalog release (Year default), one never-played catalog row (empty-state caption), one album with a pre-release play (`RuleMark` visible). LML failure injection on at least one of these to confirm the marker is suppressed gracefully.
